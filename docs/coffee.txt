Coffee originated in Ethiopia, where legend says a goat herder named Kaldi noticed his goats became energetic after eating coffee cherries. By the 15th century, coffee was being cultivated in Yemen. Arabica beans account for about 60 percent of the world's coffee production and are known for a smoother, sweeter taste. Robusta beans contain nearly twice the caffeine of Arabica and have a harsher, more bitter flavor.
