Tea is made from the leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant. Green tea, black tea, and oolong all come from the same plant and differ mainly in how much the leaves are oxidized. Black tea is fully oxidized, green tea is barely oxidized, and oolong sits in between. Tea was first consumed in China, with records dating back thousands of years. A typical cup of black tea contains about 40 milligrams of caffeine.
